LVM: Physical volume (PV)	Create with pvcreate <device>
LVM: Display physical volumes	pvdisplay

LVM: Volume group (VG)	Create with vgcreate <name> <pv...>
LVM: Display volume groups	vgdisplay
LVM: Extend volume group	vgextend <vg> <pv>

LVM: Logical volume (LV)	Create with lvcreate -L <size> -n <name> <vg>
LVM: Display logical volumes	lvdisplay
LVM: Extend logical volume	lvextend -L +<size> <lv>

LVM: Resize filesystem after extending	lvextend -r -L +<size> <lv>

LVM: Deactivate logical volume	lvchange -an <lv>
LVM: Activate logical volume	lvchange -ay <lv>

LVM: Physical volume (PV)	Physical disk or partition
LVM: Volume group (VG)	Collection of physical volumes
LVM: Logical volume (LV)	Virtual block device

LVM: Device path	/dev/<vg>/<lv>

LVM: Provides data redundancy	No
LVM: Supports online expansion	Yes
LVM: Recommended backing storage	RAID
